# Privacy Policy — Cloudflare Billing Helper for macOS

Effective date: November 5, 2026  
Developer: 肉山 (Meathill)  
Contact: meathill@gmail.com

Cloudflare Billing Helper for macOS lets you connect Cloudflare accounts to view billing estimates. This policy describes the macOS app.

## Information the app uses

To retrieve your account, subscription and usage information, the app uses the read-only Cloudflare API token you supply. The app sends requests directly to Cloudflare and stores the token in macOS Keychain. Account settings and billing snapshots are stored locally on your Mac. The app uses this information to display costs, trends and optional budget notifications.

The app does not send your token or billing data to the developer. It does not include developer telemetry or advertising analytics. Cloudflare processes the requests under its own privacy terms.

## Notifications

Budget notifications are optional. If enabled, a notification may contain an account name and an amount. You can manage notification permissions and display settings in macOS System Settings. Notifications already delivered are managed by macOS and are not automatically removed by the app.

## Removing data

When you remove an account successfully, the app deletes that account's stored token, billing cache and associated account preferences. If deletion fails, the app reports the error. You can also revoke the token in your Cloudflare account. Copies in macOS backups or other system-managed storage are not automatically removed by the app and depend on your device settings.

## External links

If you choose an external link, such as the voluntary Buy Me a Coffee support link, your browser connects to that service. The app does not collect payment information or unlock features in exchange for support. The external service has its own privacy practices.

## Contact

For questions about this policy or the macOS app, contact 肉山 (Meathill) at meathill@gmail.com.

# 隐私政策 — Cloudflare Billing Helper for macOS

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 5 日  
开发者：肉山（Meathill）  
联系方式：meathill@gmail.com

Cloudflare Billing Helper for macOS 允许你连接 Cloudflare 账户以查看账单估算。本政策适用于 macOS 应用。

## 应用使用的信息

为获取账户、订阅和用量信息，应用使用你提供的 Cloudflare 只读 API 令牌直接向 Cloudflare 发送请求。令牌保存在 macOS 钥匙串，账户设置与账单快照保存在你的 Mac 本地。应用使用这些信息展示费用、趋势和可选的预算通知。

应用不会向开发者发送令牌或账单数据，也不包含开发者遥测或广告分析。Cloudflare 按其自身隐私条款处理请求。

## 通知

预算通知为可选功能。启用后，通知可能包含账户名称和金额。你可以在 macOS 系统设置中管理通知权限和显示方式。已经送达的通知由 macOS 管理，应用不会自动清除。

## 删除数据

成功移除账户时，应用会删除该账户保存的令牌、账单缓存和相关账户偏好；如果删除失败，应用会提示错误。你也可以在 Cloudflare 账户中撤销令牌。macOS 备份或其他由系统管理的存储副本不会由应用自动清除，其处理取决于你的设备设置。

## 外部链接

如果你选择自愿支持等外部链接，浏览器会连接相应服务。应用不收集支付信息，也不会因支持而解锁功能；外部服务适用其自身隐私规则。

## 联系方式

如对本政策或 macOS 应用有疑问，请联系肉山（Meathill）：meathill@gmail.com。
