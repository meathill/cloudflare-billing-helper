# Product

## Register
product

## Users
Cloudflare account owners glance at billing while working, often across multiple accounts. They need a trustworthy view without opening a dashboard.

## Product Purpose
Show a current estimate, its components and freshness in a compact macOS menu bar utility. Make account setup, billing cycles and refresh easy to find.

## Brand Personality
Reliable, calm and concise. Use clear language and present estimates as estimates.

## Anti-references
Avoid complex dashboards, decorative charts and alarming colors for ordinary costs.

## Design Principles
- Keep the main amount and its context visible.
- Distinguish estimated values from official data and show freshness.
- Keep credentials local and give control to the account owner.
- Preserve useful cached data when a refresh fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Follow system light and dark appearance, support English and Chinese, keyboard navigation and VoiceOver.

## Internal previews
DesignPreview uses isolated sample fixtures for design QA; it is not a shipping-app feature. Real account setup uses a read-only Cloudflare API token.
